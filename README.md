## About

I'm a Full-Stack Developer and Team Lead at **Aibi Campus**, Kochi, where I lead development of **Anad CRM** — a production CRM ecosystem used for lead management and customer messaging. My work spans Flutter/Kotlin/Swift on mobile, Next.js/Node.js on the web, and the infrastructure that ties them together on DigitalOcean and Firebase.

I care about systems that hold up under real load — clean separation of concerns, sensible caching, and code that a teammate can pick up without a walkthrough.
